# Restaurant-Sales-Performance
An Excel-based restaurant sales analysis project exploring revenue performance, order trends, best-selling menu items, restaurant location performance, and customer satisfaction.

# 🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Total Revenue: $25,799.45   
Total Orders: 1,000 transactions   Total Quantity Sold: 1,755 units   Average Customer Rating: 3.9 / 5.0   

# 🎯 Key Insights & Findings
1. Dine-In generates the largest revenue contribution at 42%.
2. The Main Course category dominates the transaction volume across all order types (Delivery, Dine-In, and Takeaway).
3. Top 5 Best-Selling Menu Items (by Quantity Sold):Mushroom Risotto (Top bestseller, ~130+ units), Chicken Parmesan, NY Strip Steak, BBQ Ribs, and  Grilled Salmon.
4. Top 3 Restaurant Locations by Revenue:Downtown Manhattan, Coral Gables, and Austin South Congress.
5. Top 5 Highest-Rated Menu Items:
Sparkling Water (Highest rating, ~4.30+), Iced Coffee, Bruschetta, NY Strip Steak, and House Wine.

# Recommendations for Business Strategy
1. Given that Dine-In accounts for 42% of total revenue, focus on enhancing seating capacity and the in-house dining experience to help push customer ratings up from 3.9 towards 4.2+.
2. High-rated beverages such as Sparkling Water and Iced Coffee perform exceptionally well. Create product bundles combining these top-rated drinks with bestselling main courses like Mushroom Risotto or Chicken Parmesan.
3. Allocate marketing budgets towards Downtown Manhattan and Coral Gables, which act as the primary revenue generators.
